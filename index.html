About Us

Who we are

We are a boutique AI studio focused on building practical, agentic AI that integrates smoothly with your operations. We partner with teams to automate the repetitive, amplify human decision-making, and ship measurable improvements quickly.

Our model is deliberately small and senior — strategy, data, and engineering sit together, so we move from idea to prototype to production without hand-offs. Whether you need a lightweight agent that plugs into your workflow or a full capability build, we deliver end-to-end and own outcomes with you.

  • Agentic AI for real processes — operations, support, research, and reporting.
  • Privacy-first data handling — on-prem or VPC when required.
  • Clear metrics: time saved, quality uplift, and reliability targets.
  • Traditional ML where it wins: forecasting, scoring, and segmentation.
